Historical Timeline: Quantum Physics Journey

1900

Max Planck introduces quantization of energy.

1905

Einstein explains photoelectric effect via light quanta.

1913

Bohr model proposes quantized atomic orbits.

1925-1927

Heisenberg, Schrödinger, and Dirac establish modern quantum mechanics.

1940s+

Quantum electrodynamics (QED) and quantum chromodynamics (QCD) mature.

Modern Era

Quest for quantum gravity, grand unified theory (GUT), and theory of everything (TOE).
